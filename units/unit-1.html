Moles and Molar Mass

The mole is a fundamental unit used in chemistry to describe an amount of substance. It provides a connection between the microscopic world of particles and measurable quantities such as mass.

What is a mole?

One mole represents a specific number of particles. This allows chemists to work with extremely large numbers of atoms, molecules, and other particles using a practical unit.
